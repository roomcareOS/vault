**Goal:** turn the last ~60 days of X (Twitter) bookmarks — tools, agent tricks, interesting finds — into linked notes in this vault.

## How to get the bookmarks out

Pick whichever is easiest:

1. **X data archive (official, free).** On X: Settings → Your account → Download an archive of your data. X emails a zip within a day or two; it contains your bookmarks. Send that file (or just the bookmarks part) to Claude.
2. **Copy the links by hand.** Open your bookmarks on the phone, copy tweet links (a plain list is fine, one per line, in batches). Paste to Claude.
3. **A bookmark-export browser extension** on the Chromebook, exporting to CSV. Send the CSV to Claude.

## What happens next

Claude turns each bookmark into a note using [[Template - Bookmark Note]]: what the tool/idea is, why it helps the agentic work, and which business it could serve — wired into the graph via [[Map - Agents and Skills]].

New captures land in this `Inbox` folder first, then get filed.
