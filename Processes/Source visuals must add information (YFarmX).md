# Source visuals must add information (YFarmX)

For [[YFarmX]], every source capture should show something interesting and useful to the reader.

## What to show

Use substantive source evidence, actual hardware or event photographs, a product state, a meaningful table, a chart with its axes and legend, or a short passage that supports the reporting. A title can provide context within a larger useful capture. Never publish a screenshot containing only a title, headline, masthead or document cover. It does not satisfy the article's body-image requirement.

Capture the relevant content at its natural aspect ratio. Avoid empty webpage padding, preserve context and required attribution, link the primary source, and check readability at phone width. Identify illustrations as illustrations and keep captions within what the primary source supports.

Jay set this after rejecting title-only screenshots in the SES and FLEX articles. The FLEX replacements show ESA's fluorescence illustration and the photographed satellite launch assembly.

## Animation captions

Omit YFarmX production self-credits such as "Animation: YFarmX" and "Diagram and animation: YFarmX" beneath article animations. Keep useful explanatory captions and required third-party attribution. The site's shared banned-phrase gate rejects those self-credits in future copy.
