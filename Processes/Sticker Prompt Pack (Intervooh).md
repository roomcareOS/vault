# Sticker Prompt Pack (Intervooh)

The repeatable method for generating [[Intervooh]]'s spot illustrations: 20 sticker prompts (generated 18 July 2026), all sharing one brand base style — paste base style + subject line into any image generator. Same prompt-pack method as [[MyHomework]]'s illustration set.

## Base style (verbatim — prepended to every prompt)

> Die-cut sticker illustration with a thick white border and subtle drop shadow, flat vector style with soft rounded shapes, brand colours only: deep navy #1B2A44, mint green #4FB393, cream #FAF9F5, white. Calm, friendly, premium — no gradients, no text, no letters, isolated on a plain background.

## The 20 subjects (themes)

The two-chairs logo mark, plus interview-life objects in the brand palette: desk microphone with sound waves, circled calendar date, ticked speech bubble, trophy, handshake (navy + mint sleeves), coffee and revision notes, rising bar chart, lightbulb with a rising-path filament, clipboard checklist, blazer on a hanger, door ajar with warm light, sunrise over a path, headphones with boom mic, ten-minute kitchen timer, open notebook, thumbs-up in a shirt cuff, potted plant (growth), paper plane with dotted trail, folded map with a route to a pin.

## Usage rules (load-bearing)

- App/website spot illustrations: on the cream canvas at 96–160px; **one sticker per section** — keep the calm register, no confetti walls.
- Regenerate at 1024×1024 minimum, PNG with transparency.
- **The white border is what makes them read as a family — do not remove it.**
- **No text inside stickers** (generators misspell; the brand voice lives in copy).
