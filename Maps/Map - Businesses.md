# Map - Businesses

Every business Jay runs, and the patterns they all share. Start at [[Home]].

## The newsroom

- [[YFarmX]] — automated frontier-tech newsroom at yfarmx.com; the repo is the newsroom, every article a Markdown file, every deploy a git push.

## The care platform

- [[RoomCare]] — bedside operating system for independence and family connection in care homes; works alongside the nurse call, never in place of it.

## The learning apps

- [[MyHomework]] — homework capture, planning and AI coaching for students 13+; the coach never does the homework.
- [[Intervooh]] — interview preparation for adults: tell it company, role and date, get a day-by-day programme. Sibling of MyHomework.

## Not a business: the tools YFarmX ships

- [[Open Source Tools (YFarmX)]] — YFarmX releases small, genuinely useful open-source tools to earn stars, traffic and credibility. **They are marketing, not businesses**, and they belong to YFarmX rather than standing alone.
- [[Scenecast]] — the first of them. Free MIT-licensed command-line tool: logo and clips in, finished on-brand vertical video out, all on the user's own machine. *Currently published under the personal `roomcareOS` account; it moves to the YFarmX organisation in the org split.*

## The local service

- [[Norwich Drones]] — drone services; current focus is TikTok growth and the website. No repo yet — runs entirely on the Todoist board.

## Patterns the whole estate shares

- [[Todoist Doctrine]] — one Todoist account owns STATE across every business.
- [[Supabase Stack Pattern]] — the one proven backend recipe behind RoomCare, MyHomework and Intervooh.
- [[RLS and Schema Change Process]] — no database change without row-level security policies and a failing-read test.
