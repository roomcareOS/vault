# Research - Microsoft FY26 Model System (2026-07-30)

**What was researched:** Jay's two screenshots of Satya Nadella's post-earnings thread (FY26 Q4). Screenshots are pointers, not sources: every figure was re-established from Microsoft's own investor pages, microsoft.ai and blogs.microsoft.com. The X thread is cited nowhere in the piece.

## Key findings

- **The figures:** FY26 revenue $331.8bn (+18%); Azure +43% in Q4; capital expenditure $115.9bn for the year; the OpenAI investment swung from losses to **gains** ($4.96bn added to FY26 net income). A reported $3.2bn Anthropic gain was **left out** because it did not reconcile with the accounts — publish only what can be read off the statements.
- **The big idea — the substitutable model system.** Nadella: "the harness, context, memory, and action space are separate from any one model family." Suleyman's essay makes it doctrine: every model in a product should be swappable, over-dependence on one family is a business risk, and "token efficiency is the next big focus across the industry".
- **The proof points:** Microsoft's cheap in-house MAI Flash models replacing frontier models inside products at 50–89% cost reductions (MAI-Cyber-1-Flash topping a security benchmark at half the cost; PowerPoint image GPU costs down up to 84%); the Maia 200 inference chip giving 40% better performance per watt.
- **Copilot's arc:** "from chat to Cowork to Autopilots" — autonomous long-running agents, including an always-on personal agent powered by [[Tool - OpenClaw]]. 30m+ paid Copilot seats; revenue up 60%+ quarter-on-quarter.

## What Jay should remember

- Microsoft's harness argument is the **same doctrine [[YFarmX]] already runs**: model names in config, swappable in one place, cheap models for routine work and strong models where quality is paid for — see [[Hermes Newsroom Pipeline (YFarmX)]]. The biggest software company on earth just validated the small newsroom's architecture.
- Continuity pays: the earlier MAI-Cyber-1-Flash article (28 July) was the worked example that made the substitution claim checkable against something concrete. Prior coverage is a research asset.
- No share-price reaction is stated anywhere in the article, because the finance sites refused the container — say what could not be checked.
