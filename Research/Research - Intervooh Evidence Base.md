# Research - Intervooh Evidence Base

The cited science behind [[Intervooh]]'s coaching, from the 17 July 2026 research pass. Every claim carries an evidence tier: **[A]** meta-analysis/RCT or official source, **[B]** single peer-reviewed study or large industry survey, **[C]** practitioner consensus — directional only, never quoted numerically in paid marketing without re-verification. The rules for using any of this in public copy live in [[Marketing Claims Bank (Intervooh)]].

## The science of interview success

- **Structured interviews are the strongest single predictor of job performance** (Sackett et al. 2022 re-analysis) — ahead of cognitive ability, roughly double the validity of unstructured interviews. [A] *Implication: employers converge on exactly the format you can train for — preparing structured answers is meeting the assessment on its own terms, not gaming it.*
- **Past-behaviour questions ("tell me about a time…") beat hypotheticals** for complex professional roles. [B]
- **STAR is the assessor's own framework** (Situation, Task, Action, Result) — it mirrors how raters score. Intervooh's five rubric dimensions (Structure, Specificity, Relevance, Evidence/Impact, Delivery) map directly onto what raters reward; none requires inferring emotion.

## The science of preparation

- **Practice interviews measurably improve outcomes** — the strongest evidence found anywhere in this space. VR job-interview-training RCTs: 51% of trainees got an offer vs 25% of controls at 6 months, with odds rising with every extra practice interview (dose-response). [A] *Caveat: trials were in populations facing employment barriers — say "strongly linked to better outcomes", never "guarantees offers".*
- **Anxiety costs real marks**: anxiety→performance correlation r = −.19; mock settings reliably reduce state anxiety. [A]
- **Retrieval practice confirmed**: practising answers out loud beats re-reading notes (g = 0.61 overall across 217 studies; 0.51 vs re-studying). [A] — the same finding underpinning [[MyHomework]]'s coaching.
- **Spacing beats cramming**, and the ideal gap scales with the runway (317 experiments). [A] *These two findings are why the programme engine spreads story rehearsal across ≥3 separate days and escalates mocks toward the date.*

## Delivery mechanics: measured vs refused

**Measured (behavioural, countable):** filler rate (target under ~5% of words), pace (credible delivery ≈120–160 words per minute; ~130 reduces fillers), answer length (1.5–2.5 minutes suits STAR). [B/C]

**Refused:** inferred emotion, "confidence scores", personality readings from face or voice. The famous "93% of communication is non-verbal" figure is a misreading of Mehrabian's narrow 1967 experiments — a misuse he himself disavowed. Coach observable mechanics; never claim to read minds. [A/B]

## UK landscape, 2026

- **Most competitive graduate market on record**: 140 applications per vacancy (up 59% in one year, highest since 1991); retail/FMCG ~290. Graduate hiring falling. [B] — date-stamp this stat in any copy.
- **Competency-based and strengths-based interviews dominate UK formats.** Strengths interviews reward quick authentic answers, a distinct prep mode for graduate users. [B/C]
- **Civil Service Success Profiles**: nine named Behaviours, listed per advert — preparing means mapping stories to those exact names. [A — official]
- **NHS values-based recruitment**: six NHS Constitution values, unchanged as of 2026. [A — official]
- **Regional norms differ** (UK understatement vs US energy; German formality; French relationship focus; legal red lines vary by country) — region is a first-class input in the [[Question Database (Intervooh)]], UK default.

## AI and the 2026 interview

- AI is standard hiring infrastructure (~96% of hiring professionals use it somewhere). [C]
- **Async one-way video interviews are mainstream at the top of the funnel** — talking to a camera with no human feedback is a distinct skill the voice-first mocks train. [C]
- **The verification backlash**: because AI makes async assessment gameable, employers add live human rounds — genuine fluency matters more, not less. [C]
- ~22% of candidates admit using AI live during interviews — the practice Intervooh publicly refuses to enable; ~70% of employers now probe candidates' AI fluency, so every job's bank carries era-2026 questions. [C]

## Compliance (resolved)

**EU AI Act Article 5(1)(f)** prohibits AI that infers emotions in workplace contexts, explicitly including recruitment — in force since February 2025, fines to €35M or 7% of global turnover. Intervooh's position: candidate-side, self-serve practice tool; assesses nobody for employers; infers no emotion (the edge-function guard enforces this — see [[Decisions - Intervooh]]). Counsel review before EU launch stays on the checklist.
